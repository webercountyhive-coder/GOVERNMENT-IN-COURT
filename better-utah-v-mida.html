Who governs Stratos? Alliance for a Better Utah v. MIDA

A nonprofit and anonymous Box Elder County residents say the Military Installation Development Authority's board and powers break the Utah Constitution, and ask a court to stop the Stratos data center project area plan.

Court: Third District (Salt Lake)Filed June 3, 2026Amended Sept. 28, 2026Status: no ruling found

Posted Nov. 6, 2026

WHERE · WHO
Military Installation Development Authority (MIDA) · Box Elder County Commission · Third District Court
  • Who is suing: Alliance for a Better Utah and Jane Does 1-5 (Box Elder County residents). Better Utah's case page also lists Saline Now, Inc. and another unnamed nonprofit.
  • Who is being sued: MIDA, Sen. J. Stuart Adams, Sen. Jerry Stevenson and the Box Elder County Commission.
  • Lawyers: David R. Irvine (Bountiful) and Alan L. Smith (Salt Lake City), for the plaintiffs.
  • What it is about: Whether MIDA, with sitting legislators on its board, can exercise taxing and land-use powers over the Stratos project area.

In plain words (explain it like I'm 10)

MIDA is a state authority run by an appointed board, not by voters. Two of its board members were sitting state senators. The people suing say the Utah Constitution doesn't let lawmakers also sit on a board like that, and doesn't let an unelected board use powers like taxing. They want a judge to say so and stop the Stratos data center plan. No judge has ruled yet.

The short version

  • Filed: June 3, 2026, in Third District Court. The case number and judge are blank on the public copy of the complaint.
  • Ten claims, including Art. VI §6 (legislators holding another office) and Art. VI §28 (giving taxing or city powers to a special commission).
  • What they ask: declare the MIDA act and actions void, permanently block the Stratos Project Area Plan, declare Adams and Stevenson "unconstitutional appointees," and order Adams to "disgorge monies."
  • Status: KSL/Deseret reported Oct. 2, 2026, that the defendants "hadn't yet responded." The Hive has not checked the docket since. An amended complaint was filed Sept. 28 (Better Utah).

What court rulings and records confirm

  • The complaint was filed June 3, 2026, by attorneys David R. Irvine and Alan L. Smith (complaint).
  • Named defendants: MIDA, Stuart Adams, Jerry Stevenson and the Box Elder County Commission. The State of Utah is not named (complaint).
  • No motions or rulings were reported as of Oct. 2, 2026 (KSL/Deseret; Better Utah case page, status date Sept. 30). This does not show there has been no response since.

Claims no court has decided

These are what a party says in its filings. They are not findings.

  • The complaint alleges ten violations, including abridging direct and representative democracy rights, unconstitutional board composition, Art. VI §28 and §29 violations, non-compliance with the MIDA Act, and breach of fiduciary duty.
  • Better Utah alleges "Adams accepted $135,000 as a PAC donation from MIDA contractors days after the county greenlit the project." No court has decided this.
