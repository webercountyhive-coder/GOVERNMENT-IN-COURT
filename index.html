Government in Court: when Utah agencies and officials are taken to court

A docket of court cases where a Utah government, agency or official is sued or challenged, read from the filings. Each case shows who is suing whom, what each side says, what records confirm, and where it stands.

Posted Nov. 6, 2026 · Updated Nov. 6, 2026

In plain words

Sometimes people take their government to court, and sometimes one government sues another. This page keeps a list of those cases. A lawsuit is one side's story until a judge or jury decides, so every case here keeps what each side says apart from what the records show.
